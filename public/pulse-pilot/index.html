Simple controls.
Plenty to get better at.

There’s no steering wheel. Tap below or beside the pilot to push it where you want it to go. The trick is placing the next pulse before the next gate catches you out.

You don’t tap the pilot. You move the space around it.

Tap anywhere to create a pulse that pushes the pilot away. A pulse below lifts it. A pulse beside it changes its direction. The placement matters.

How do the controls work?

Tap the screen to make an energy pulse. The pulse pushes the pilot away from where you tapped. Use its position to change your direction.

How do I learn to play?

Start with the interactive tutorial. It introduces pulse placement, slowing down, gate centers, and power-ups.

What is a perfect chain?

Passing through the center of a gate counts as a perfect pass. Keep landing those passes to build your chain.

Does the game save my best score?

Yes. Your high score is saved so you can try to beat it on your next run.

Is Pulse Pilot free?

The Android game is free to download and contains ads. An optional one-time purchase removes the ads.
