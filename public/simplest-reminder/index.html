What repeat schedules are supported?

Daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, weekdays, and weekends. You can also set a custom interval in hours, days, weeks, months, or years.

What’s the difference between snooze and repeat?

Snooze postpones an alert you’ve received. Repeat schedules future occurrences of the reminder, such as every Monday.

What if a reminder doesn’t appear?

Check notification permission, precise alarm access, and battery restrictions on your device. Android settings can affect when alerts appear. If you’re still having trouble, get in touch with your device model and Android version.

Can I back up my reminders?

Yes. The app supports exporting reminders to a backup file and restoring them from a file.

Is the app free?

The Android app is free to download and contains ads. An optional one-time purchase removes the ads.
